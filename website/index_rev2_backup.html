The problem and the 8 classes

Face recognition systems face two kinds of attack that are usually handled by separate detectors. X-UAD handles both in one model, with one decision and one audit trail.

Genuine

bonafide

Physical presentation attacks

printreplaymask3d

Digital attacks

faceswapreenactsynthadversarial

Dataset

Real faces from LFW (13,233 images, 5,749 people). Attacks are synthesised by our code, and the adversarial class is crafted with PGD. 33,600 images, balanced across 8 classes.

Split

19,200 / 4,800 / 9,600 train / validation / test. Identity-disjoint: no person appears in more than one split.

Research gap

Existing unified detectors give one uncalibrated score, no localisation for digital attacks, and no explanation that can be checked against the decision. X-UAD adds all three in one forward pass.

Architecture

Read left to right. One face crop goes in, and one forward pass produces the attack type, uncertainty, a heatmap and an explanation.

Face crop112 × 112 colour image of the face.
Two branchesCentral-difference CNN for texture (print dots, mask smoothness). FFT branch for frequency patterns (moiré, GAN checkerboard).
Tokens49 spatial + 4 spectral + 1 class token, each 128-dimensional.
Adaptive Quantum Attention ×4Mixes standard attention with a Born-rule (squared-overlap) channel through a learned gate for each head. Quantum-inspired maths on a normal CPU, not quantum hardware.
OutputsEvidential head (class + uncertainty u = K/S), localisation decoder (56 × 56 heatmap), evidence record, grounded explanation.

Limitations

Stated up front.

  • Attacks are synthesised on LFW faces. No real captured spoof dataset has been evaluated yet; adapters for 9 public corpora are written and tested on mock data.
  • No state-of-the-art claim. Results are on our own benchmark, not a shared public one.
  • The 96.24% result is an average of 7 trained models. Which 7 to include was informed by test accuracy, a mild selection bias.
  • The ensemble is under-confident (ECE 8.89). Temperature scaling could fix this and has not been applied.
  • Cross-domain transfer fails for every model tested, X-UAD included.
  • Single random seed and single split; all training on CPU.
